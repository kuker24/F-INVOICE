# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: a single **Developer-owner** who runs one business — projects, hosting, maintenance, and app subscriptions — and needs one source of truth for balances, proofs, and overdue status. They work at a desk or laptop, often after client calls, under normal office light, in a task-first mood (get invoice out, confirm payment, chase overdue).

Secondary: **Admin** ops staff who execute day-to-day invoicing and payment verification without touching system secrets or business root settings; and **User** (customer portal) who only views their own invoices, subscriptions, and payment confirmations.

## Product Purpose

F-INVOICE is a **private, invite-only** invoice management system for one business root. It covers customers, products/services, invoices, subscriptions, payments (manual proof + verify), PDF, public token links, activity log, and notifications. Success is: staff ship and settle invoices without spreadsheets; customers pay via a controlled public or portal path; money stays integer IDR with an auditable status machine.

## Positioning

One owner, one ledger — invite-only ops, not multi-tenant SaaS.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, clinical, operator-grade. Voice is Indonesian product UI: short labels, clear status words, no marketing fluff. Feels like developer infrastructure on frosted paper — confident without shouting.

## Anti-references

- Public SaaS onboarding, pricing pages, or multi-tenant “workspaces”
- Navy-and-gold fintech chrome, emerald growth dashboards, gradient hero metrics
- Glassmorphism, side-stripe cards, AI-cream marketing landing as the app shell
- Dark-first “cool tools” skin as the default identity (dark is optional system preference only)
- Payment-gateway marketplace UX or WhatsApp-chat product surfaces in MVP

## Design Principles

1. **Task over theatre** — every screen serves a job (list, create, verify, share); no decorative chrome.
2. **One vocabulary** — same table chrome, empty states, buttons, and status badges everywhere.
3. **Money is sacred** — tabular IDR, integer math, status machine visible and honest.
4. **Invite-only clarity** — no public signup; empty and error states teach the next staff action.
5. **Fast on free tier** — Hobby constraints are product constraints: snappy warm paths beat perfect security theatre.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for interactive surfaces: labeled forms, visible focus, skip link, semantic landmarks, keyboard-usable nav. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Body and mid-gray text must stay readable on canvas/paper in light and dark. Indonesian is the primary UI language.
